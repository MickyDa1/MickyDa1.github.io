Introduction

I am a hardworking and motivated Software Engineering student with a strong interest in building a career within the software industry. I enjoy taking on new challenges and am committed to giving my full effort to every project and opportunity. Alongside my university coursework, I regularly undertake personal projects to expand my technical knowledge and gain practical experience with new technologies.

Throughout my studies, I have developed a passion for software engineering and have continuously learned techniques and tools with real-world applications. Modules such as "Software Engineering Workshop" and "Data Structures and Algorithms" have challenged me to think critically, solve complex problems, and collaborate effectively as part of a team.

I am always looking for opportunities to broaden my skill set, whether by learning new programming languages, exploring emerging technologies, or developing projects that allow me to apply theoretical knowledge in practical environments. My goal is to continue growing as a software engineer while contributing to meaningful and innovative solutions.


Career Goals

My long-term career goal is to establish my own business specialising in the design and development of bespoke Internet of Things (IoT) solutions. I aspire to create innovative hardware and software products that are tailored to the specific requirements of individual clients, combining my passion for embedded systems, software engineering, and problem-solving.

Before pursuing this goal, I plan to spend the first 10–15 years of my career working within the software engineering industry. This will allow me to gain valuable commercial experience, develop my technical and professional skills, understand the needs of the market, and build a strong network of industry contacts. It will also provide financial stability while I repay my student loans and prepare for launching my own business.

My Plan of Action to achieve this:

  • Complete my university studies by continuing to build a strong foundation in software engineering principles and practical development.
  • Secure a full-time software engineering role where I can gain experience working on real-world projects, collaborate with experienced professionals, and continue developing my technical expertise.
  • stablish my own IoT business, applying the knowledge and experience gained throughout my career to deliver bespoke solutions for clients.
  • Continue learning and growing throughout my career, adapting to new technologies and industry trends while building a successful and sustainable business.
